Yimin Du · First submitted 2 June 2025 · arXiv:2507.07107

About the paper

The paper studies how non-tradable limit-move observations can enter rolling factors before portfolio selection. It presents a mask-first factor pipeline and a cross-sectional research stack covering factor construction, model baselines, portfolio optimization, and backtesting.

The repository provides synthetic and public-data research paths. The paper's proprietary A-share panel is not redistributed; historical public-data results and their protocol limitations are recorded in the validation dashboard.
